Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: August 1, 2025

Do It Now ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Desejos Studio. This policy explains what information the Do It Now mobile application ("App") collects, how it is used, and what it cannot see or access.

1. No User Accounts

Do It Now does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or register in any way. All your habit data — your schedules, challenge history, selected apps, and intentions — is stored exclusively on your device in a local database. There is no cloud sync and no backend account associated with you.

2. Device Permissions We Request

The App requests the following permissions. We only request what is strictly necessary to deliver the core intervention experience.

Permission Why it is needed
Microphone To detect when you say the challenge phrase. Audio is processed in real time on-device and is never recorded or stored.
Speech Recognition To count repetitions of the spoken challenge phrase using the operating system's built-in speech framework.
Reminders (optional) If you choose to save a post-challenge intention, the App can create a reminder on your device. It can only write reminders you create — it cannot read your existing reminders or calendar.
Siri / Shortcuts (optional, iOS) Lets you trigger the daily challenge via a Siri Shortcut automation you configure yourself. No data is shared with Siri.
Screen Time / FamilyControls (iOS 16+) Apple's official Screen Time framework lets you choose which apps to protect. The App only learns the app bundle IDs you select, and only when you attempt to open one of them.
Accessibility Service (Android, optional) Used to detect when you open a selected app on Android. The service reads only the foreground app package name. It cannot read screen content, notifications, keystrokes, messages, or any activity inside other apps.
Photo Library (iOS) Do It Now does not access your photos. This permission declaration is required by a third-party dependency but is never used by the App itself.

3. What the App Stores Locally

The following data is stored on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Your intervention windows (time ranges and active days)
  • Which apps you have selected to protect
  • Your daily challenge counts, completion status, and timestamps
  • Your challenge sessions (start/end times, counts)
  • Intentions you type after completing a challenge
  • Your 7-day trial start and end times
  • A cached record of your current subscription status
  • Your analytics opt-in preference

This data lives in a SQLite database on your device. Uninstalling the App removes it entirely.

4. What the App Cannot See

These data types are never accessed, recorded, or transmitted — by design:

  • The content of your messages, emails, or social media feeds
  • What you do inside any other app
  • Your contacts, photos, location, or browsing history
  • Your keystrokes or screen content
  • Your microphone audio (the App receives only real-time text from the OS speech framework; audio never enters the App's code)
  • Your speech transcripts (used only for instant phrase counting and never persisted)
  • Your existing reminders or calendar events
  • Your notifications

5. Analytics (Opt-In Only)

Analytics are disabled by default. You are asked during onboarding whether you wish to share anonymous usage data to help improve the App. You can change this preference at any time in Settings.

If you opt in, we use PostHog to collect anonymous event data such as: challenge completed, paywall viewed, app opened. Each event is tagged with a randomly generated identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or any personal information. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) and PostHog is configured not to store your IP address.

Your habit data, app selections, intentions, voice audio, and speech transcripts are never sent to PostHog.

PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy

6. Crash Reporting (Sentry)

We use Sentry to receive anonymised error and crash reports. This helps us identify and fix technical problems. Reports contain only error details, diagnostic state (e.g. subscription status tag, which feature was active), and a stable anonymous identifier for error correlation. They never contain user-entered content, task text, intention text, audio, or any personal information. Our code enforces these exclusions explicitly.

Sentry's privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy

7. Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app-specific user identifier (not your name or email) and your subscription status from the App Store or Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google and are never seen by Do It Now or RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

8. Speech Recognition and Third-Party Platforms

Voice challenge recognition uses the native speech framework provided by your device's operating system — SFSpeechRecognizer on iOS and SpeechRecognizer on Android. These platform APIs may route audio to Apple's or Google's servers for processing. Do It Now never receives, stores, or transmits your audio. The platform APIs return only a text transcript which the App uses in real time to count phrase repetitions and then discards.

For details on how Apple and Google handle speech data, see their respective privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the App, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

All local data is retained on your device until you uninstall the App. Anonymous analytics events sent to PostHog are retained according to PostHog's retention policy. Sentry crash reports are retained for a limited diagnostic period. Because we hold no personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on request — uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

11. Your Rights

Because Do It Now holds no personal data on its own servers, most data rights (access, rectification, erasure) are exercised directly on your device by managing or uninstalling the App. For any questions or concerns about analytics or crash report data, contact us at the address below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be indicated by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, via an in-app notice. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
