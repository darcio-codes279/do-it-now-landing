Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 3, 2026

Do It Now ("we", "our", or "us") is operated by Desejos Studio Ltd, which is the controller of the personal data described here. This policy explains what information the Do It Now mobile application ("App") collects, how it is used, who processes it, and what the App cannot see or access.

1. Your Account

Using the App requires an account. Your account lets us back up your setup and history, restore them on a new or reinstalled phone, and link your subscription to you rather than to a single device. You can sign in with an email address and password, with Sign in with Apple, or with Google.

When you create or use an account we process:

  • Your email address and whether you have verified it. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive Apple's private relay address instead.
  • A display name. For email sign-up this is the part of your email address before the "@"; for Apple or Google it is the name the provider shares, if any.
  • Your password, in hashed form only, if you sign up with email. We never store or see your password in plain text.
  • Your Apple or Google account identifier and the sign-in token the provider issues, if you use Apple or Google. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Session records: a session token, its expiry, and the IP address and device type (user agent) used to sign in. Sessions last up to 90 days.
  • Security records: short-lived counters keyed to your IP address that limit repeated sign-in attempts.

We use this information only to run your account, keep it secure, send you account emails (email verification and password reset), and restore your data. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

2. Website Beta Signups

If you join the beta through this website, we collect the email address you provide so we can contact you about beta access and testing. Website signup data is separate from the mobile App and is not used to identify or personalize your local App data. We do not sell signup information or use it for unrelated advertising. You can request removal from the beta list by contacting us at desejos.studio@proton.me.

3. Device Permissions We Request

The App requests the following permissions. We only request what is strictly necessary to deliver the core intervention experience.

Permission Why it is needed
Microphone To detect when you say the challenge phrase. Audio is processed in real time on-device and is never recorded or stored.
Speech Recognition To count repetitions of the spoken challenge phrase using the operating system's built-in speech framework.
Reminders (optional) If you choose to save a post-challenge intention, the App can create a reminder on your device. It can only write reminders you create — it cannot read your existing reminders or calendar.
Screen Time / FamilyControls (iOS 16+) Apple's official Screen Time framework lets you choose which apps to protect. The App only learns the app bundle IDs you select, and only when you attempt to open one of them.
Accessibility Service (Android, optional) Used to detect when a selected app enters the foreground so Do It Now can start your pause challenge. The service listens only for window-change events and reads only the foreground app package name to compare it with the apps you selected. This information is used on-device, stored locally, and is not sent to or shared with Do It Now, Desejos Studio, or third parties. The service cannot read screen content, text fields, notifications, keystrokes, messages, or activity inside other apps, and it cannot perform gestures or actions in other apps. You can turn this access off at any time in Android Settings.

4. Android Accessibility Data

If you enable the optional Android Accessibility Service, Do It Now receives window-change events while the service is active. It uses the package name associated with an event only to determine whether the app entering the foreground is one you selected for protection and whether to start your pause challenge.

The service does not retrieve window content or accessibility nodes. It does not read or record messages, notifications, screen content, text fields, keystrokes, passwords, or media, and it does not perform gestures or actions inside other apps. Accessibility data used for this feature remains on your device and is not transmitted, shared, or used for advertising. You may disable the service at any time through Android Settings. If you do not enable it, the rest of the App remains usable, although automatic Android intervention will not operate.

5. Account Backup

While you are signed in, the App backs up the following to your account so you can restore it on any phone:

  • Your setup: your promise windows (time ranges and active days), the apps you selected to protect (their identifiers and names), your challenge settings, and your preferences, including your analytics choice
  • Your history: daily challenge counts, completion status and timestamps, and challenge sessions (start and end times, counts)

The backup is updated when you sign in, when you sign out, and when the App goes to the background. Signing out backs up your data and then removes it from the phone; signing back in restores it.

6. What Stays Only on Your Phone

The following is never sent to our servers or included in your backup:

  • Intentions you type after completing a challenge, and your tasks
  • Your voice audio and speech transcripts
  • A cached record of your current subscription status

This data lives in a SQLite database on your device. Signing out, deleting your account, or uninstalling the App removes it.

7. What the App Cannot See

These data types are never accessed, recorded, or transmitted — by design:

  • The content of your messages, emails, or social media feeds
  • What you do inside any other app
  • Your contacts, photos, location, or browsing history
  • Your keystrokes or screen content
  • Your microphone audio (the App receives only real-time text from the OS speech framework; audio never enters the App's code)
  • Your speech transcripts (used only for instant phrase counting and never persisted)
  • Your existing reminders or calendar events
  • Your notifications

8. Analytics (Opt-In Only)

Analytics are disabled by default. You are asked during onboarding whether you wish to share anonymous usage data to help improve the App. You can change this preference at any time in Settings.

If you opt in, we use PostHog to collect anonymous usage data such as challenge completion, paywall views, feature interactions, app version, operating system, and event timestamps. Each event is tagged with a randomly generated identifier that is not linked to your account, name, email, Apple ID, Google account, or any personal information. We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), use this data for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. PostHog is configured not to store your IP address.

Your habit data, app selections, intentions, voice audio, and speech transcripts are never sent to PostHog.

PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy

9. Crash Reporting (Sentry, Opt-In Only)

Crash diagnostics are disabled by default and are enabled only when you opt in to anonymous analytics and diagnostics during onboarding or in Settings. When enabled, Sentry receives anonymised error and crash reports to help us identify and fix technical problems. Reports may contain error details, app version, operating system, diagnostic state (for example, subscription status or the feature that was active), and a stable anonymous identifier for error correlation. Session Replay is disabled. Reports do not contain your account identifier, email address, user-entered content, task text, intention text, audio, speech transcripts, app names, or advertising identifiers.

Sentry's privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy

10. Subscriptions (RevenueCat)

Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat. When you sign in, the App identifies you to RevenueCat by your Do It Now account identifier, so your subscription follows your account to any phone. RevenueCat receives that identifier, your purchase history, product and purchase status, entitlement status, and information needed to validate and manage the subscription. We do not send RevenueCat your email address or name. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple or Google and are never seen by Do It Now or RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

11. Speech Recognition and Third-Party Platforms

Voice challenge recognition uses the native speech framework provided by your device's operating system — SFSpeechRecognizer on iOS and SpeechRecognizer on Android. These platform APIs may route audio to Apple's or Google's servers for processing. Do It Now never receives, stores, or transmits your audio. The platform APIs return only a text transcript which the App uses in real time to count phrase repetitions and then discards.

For details on how Apple and Google handle speech data, see their respective privacy policies.

13. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information through the App, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

14. Data Retention and Deletion

Your account and backup are kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Account → Delete account. This immediately deletes your account, your sessions, and your backup from our servers and removes the App's data from your phone. Deleted data may remain in our encrypted database backups for up to 30 days before it is overwritten. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription; manage that in your App Store or Google Play account. Session records expire after 90 days, and security counters are short-lived.

Data on your phone is kept until you sign out, delete your account, or uninstall the App. Anonymous analytics events sent to PostHog are retained for up to 12 months. Sentry crash reports are retained for a limited diagnostic period. RevenueCat retains subscription and purchase records for as long as needed to provide subscription management and meet applicable legal, accounting, and platform requirements. You can stop future analytics and diagnostics collection at any time in Settings. For requests concerning data held by PostHog, Sentry, or RevenueCat, contact us and we will coordinate the request with the relevant provider.

15. Legal Bases

Where data protection law such as the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Performing our contract with you: running your account, backing up and restoring your data, sending account emails, and managing your subscription.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping accounts secure, preventing abuse such as repeated sign-in attempts, and fixing problems.
  • Consent: anonymous analytics and crash reports, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

16. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can delete your account and backup yourself in the App. For any other request, email us at the address below; we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
